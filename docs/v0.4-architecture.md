# V0.7 Architecture

Council of Translation separates its FastMCP boundary from a testable Council Core. FastMCP adapters implement model sampling and user elicitation; scripted adapters exercise the same orchestration without Goose or live models.

## Pipeline

Before the numbered review pipeline, a sampling-free Briefing Gate builds a bounded
`ReviewBriefV2`. `briefing_mode=auto` asks source/candidate-only callers before the
first sample, rich context skips, `always` asks and stops before sampling on non-accept,
and `off` records explicit assumptions. Accepted mutable answers retain
`user_briefing` provenance; caller hard constraints remain intact. Content aliases such
as `UI button` normalize deterministically before role routing.

1. Deterministic preflight extracts named/braced/printf placeholders, variables, commands, tags, URLs, explicit do-not-translate literals, and machine-readable caller hard constraints. Placeholder, required token, markup, URL, and explicit literal loss can block. Numeric and Markdown differences are warnings unless promoted by `numeric_parity` or `markdown_parity`; `required_literal:<text>` and `forbidden_literal:<text>` enforce exact caller requirements. Other free-form TB/SG/project/technical packets guide reviewers but cannot become deterministic blockers by model assertion alone.
2. `CouncilPlan` selects executable reviewer roles by mode and content type. The chief editor is an adjudicator, not an independent reviewer.
3. Independent role calls return bounded natural `role_feedback` plus conservative `FindingV2` evidence classified as `issue`, `choice`, or `affirmation`. Concrete `proposed_value` is the only model proposal eligible to become an outcome; `action` remains execution advice. Model output cannot create hard constraints or blockers. Optional `context_gaps` are parsed independently: malformed gaps are counted without erasing valid findings. Core deduplicates material unanswered gaps, asks at most two in one flat follow-up, and reconsiders at most three affected roles when actual answers are accepted. Gap evidence cannot create hard constraints or blockers.
4. Findings are clustered by normalized issue family and text anchors. Affirmations count toward reviewer coverage but do not create clusters. Harmless Unicode/whitespace variants collapse, while materially distinct proposed values remain separate. Production logic contains no named-example conflict shortcuts.
5. Standard and strict modes may run one discussion round: standard considers at most one issue/three roles; strict at most two issues/four roles. Lightweight skips discussion. A declared position change is applied only for an allowed issue/role and an existing candidate action; model discussion cannot create a blocker or hard tier. The updated matrix is used downstream.
6. A single deterministic issue/outcome identity is shared by outcome-backed RolePositions and DecisionPoint options. Only a validated `choice` finding with a non-empty bounded `proposed_value` contributes a proposed outcome; `action` prose never does, including legacy or incomplete classifications. A consistent bounded cluster `candidate_span` becomes the issue-local current outcome; the complete candidate document is never displayed as an option. When known, the valid current outcome is first, followed by at most two alternatives; fewer than two distinct valid outcomes produces no point. Each local outcome is validated by replacing one provable anchor in the complete candidate and rerunning deterministic checks on that reconstruction. Missing or repeated anchors are rejected without guessing or replacing all occurrences, persisted as bounded content-free Policy Gate suppression provenance, and surfaced as compact degradation; normal hard-constraint rejection remains non-degraded validation. Up to three points are collected in one standard elicitation form. Concise human-readable enum values map per field to internal option IDs; collision disambiguation remains readable. Explicit Council delegation is appended last as an interaction action, not a Position outcome.
7. Accepted outcomes trigger one call per contrary/materially affected role, capped at three. Supporting roles are not resampled solely for agreement. Roles are prioritized by blocking expertise, evidence tier/provenance, configured priority, and deterministic tie-break. `continue_review` deep-copies the prior record, links `parent_review_id`, and does not rerun independent reviews.
8. The Policy Gate protects immutable constraints. Position scoring supports role relevance, evidence provenance, constraint tier, blocking state, evidence/rule references, and confidence. Production language-choice findings are model-origin and therefore advisory/non-blocking; deterministic caller and preflight constraints are enforced separately before preferences become DecisionPoints. Duplicate same-role/action findings collapse to one matrix row, and each role's fixed total influence is normalized across distinct actions, so repetition cannot become voting weight. Non-tied evidence can select a fallback; tied or insufficient evidence requests human review. Role counts are not a decision rule. Chief output resolves selected IDs to readable actions while DecisionTrace retains the structured outcome and basis.

## Safety and budgets

All MCP payloads and model outputs are untrusted. Prompts delimit data, request structured claims/evidence instead of hidden reasoning, and constrain output sizes. Malformed samples degrade to non-blocking missing evidence.

Call budgets are enforced at 6/13/18 for lightweight/standard/strict. Briefing and elicitation do not consume sampling budget. A deep standard path fits six independent reviews, three context reconsiderations, one discussion, and three outcome reconsiderations exactly within 13. There is at most one context follow-up form, two context questions, one outcome form, three DecisionPoints, and three calls in each reconsideration phase. Requested, completed, skipped, failed and bounded change effects are persisted separately per phase. A skipped or failed required reconsideration sets `degraded=true`, emits bounded warnings, and prevents an unqualified `COMPLETED` status.

The structured response retains the deterministic 12-field `ProcessDigestV2`: case
brief; assumptions/confidence; blind spots; role lenses; consensus; minority report and
decisive counterfactual; material disagreements; context gaps/answers; user decisions;
reconsideration changes; editor synthesis; and execution checklist/final disposition.
Every active role retains a lens. A presentation adapter renders those fields as an
adaptive Chinese report with no more than five sections, a clean-case target of 1,800
Unicode code points, and a hard cap of 3,200. Empty interaction sections are omitted;
blockers, dissent, conditions, coverage gaps, warnings and degradation are preserved;
canonical adjudication counters and redundant clean-role evidence stay out of primary
text, while optional material evidence is either complete or omitted. The structured
digest retains the full chief rationale and role evidence. The final editor disposition
is last. It never invents hidden reasoning or a full
translation in review-only mode. A 13-phase trace records bounded disposition/count
facts rather than reasoning.

At the FastMCP boundary, `review_translation`, `continue_review`, and
`view_review_record` return the report as the first text content block and the unchanged
compact/full dictionary as structured content. This keeps human presentation concise
without discarding machine-readable evidence or changing Council adjudication.

Independent-review coverage requires semantic envelope validation after JSON decoding: both required keys, string `role_feedback`, list `findings`, and safely validated object entries. A zero-finding response succeeds only with non-whitespace feedback; blank feedback is allowed when a valid finding remains. The deterministic conservative policy discards all findings from a sample if any entry is invalid and marks that sample unavailable. Transport errors, malformed/reasoning-only or empty content, invalid JSON, missing/wrong envelope fields, inert findings, and finding validation failures are unavailable samples with bounded schema provenance. Full coverage can complete normally; partial or zero coverage conservatively requires human review and records successful/unavailable counts plus `full`/`partial`/`none` coverage. No retry calls are added. This is Core behavior verified with local deterministic adapters; it is not a claim of live provider verification.

## Storage

`ReviewStore` writes schema 2.2 with atomic replacement and validates record IDs before access. Write/replace failures become a path-redacted `ReviewPersistenceError`. `full`, `metadata`, and `off` privacy modes are explicit. Metadata persistence is allowlist-based: it retains safe status/publishability/review-needed disposition, guided-session modes/counts/confidence and content-free role IDs, while omitting source, candidate, rule packets, briefing/context answers, display Markdown, model/user/chief prose, and secrets. V2.2, V2.1, V2.0, and V1 remain readable; legacy records without `schema_version` parse as V1.
